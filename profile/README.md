# Welcome to Oystr Dev!

We are a team of developers dedicated to developing and maintaining a private Minecraft server and its infrastructure.

## What we do

Our main focus is creating an exceptional Minecraft experience for our players. We continuously work on improving server performance, adding new features, and fixing bugs.

## Our projects

We are gradually open-sourcing parts of our stack. Some of our tools and components will be available publicly, and we plan to publish more projects over time.

## Contact

We are not actively recruiting new team members right now, but if you're interested in our development process, feel free to reach out on Discord: @mean.ing. We're happy to answer any questions.

Thanks for your interest in Oystr Dev — we hope to hear from you soon.
